FoodTraceAI demo terms

FoodTraceAI is a hackathon demonstration by AWS-25. Supplier inventory, provenance, preparation times and cost assumptions are synthetic. Recommendations do not constitute food-safety clearance or create real orders, holds, reservations or dispatches.

The Traffic & timing preview uses Google Maps Routes estimates for future departure times. Road and traffic estimates are not guaranteed arrival times. By using these Google Maps features, you are bound by the Google Maps / Google Earth Additional Terms of Service. Google’s Privacy Policy applies to Google’s services.
