FoodTraceAI demo privacy

Use fictional reports only; do not enter patient details or other sensitive personal information. Incident reports, reviewed evidence, operational records and saved plans are stored in our AWS DynamoDB demo backend. The shared access code is stored in your browser session.

Amazon Bedrock processes report and planning inputs. For Traffic & timing, Google Maps receives source and destination coordinates and requested departure times through the server. Bedrock receives aggregate alternative metrics, incident receipt time and planning constraints to recommend an option. The Google API key stays on the backend.

Traffic responses and the resulting comparison are not written to the app’s incident records, plan history, exports or browser storage. The preview exists in memory while its screen is open and is cleared after 15 minutes or when you leave. Infrastructure providers may process request metadata under their own terms.
